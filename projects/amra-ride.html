Overview

Amra RIDE is a rideshare and local delivery platform with a clear proposition: rideshare, not revenue share. Riders get an upfront fare and ETA before they book, drivers keep more of what they earn, and every trip is confirmed with a PIN at pickup.

The product is split into three surfaces: a marketing site that explains the model and recruits drivers, the rider and driver apps (coming soon on the App Store and Google Play), and an operations dashboard where the team watches the fleet in real time, approves drivers, and handles safety alerts. All three sit on the same Laravel REST API.

  • Upfront fares and live ETAs before a ride is confirmed.
  • Smart dispatch that matches each request to a nearby available driver.
  • Rides and door-to-door package delivery from the same platform.
  • Fleet management for drivers, fleet owners, and vehicles.
  • Live operations: fleet map, heat map, SOS alerts, live chat, and complaints.
  • Zones, airports, service locations, promo codes, and payment mix reporting.

My role

I built the ride-hailing APIs and worked on the operations dashboard that the team uses to run the network.

  • Designed and implemented Laravel REST endpoints for ride requests, dispatch, trips, drivers, riders, fleets, and vehicles.
  • Modelled zones, service locations, and trip state transitions in MySQL.
  • Implemented trip PIN verification and returned upfront fare and ETA data to the client apps.
  • Built dashboard views for the live trip feed, driver approvals, and emergency SOS alerts.
  • Supported the payment mix — card/online, cash, and wallet — plus promo codes and commission tracking.

Key features

Upfront fares & trip PIN

Riders see the fare and arrival time before booking, then confirm the ride with a four-digit PIN at pickup.

Smart dispatch

Each request goes straight to available drivers nearby so matches happen quickly and automatically.

Rides & delivery

Everyday rides, city-to-city and hourly trips, plus door-to-door package delivery by local drivers.

Fleet management

Drivers, fleet drivers, fleet owners, vehicles, and approval queues managed from one place.

Live operations

A live fleet map, heat map, emergency SOS alerts, live chat, and complaint handling in real time.

Pricing & zones

Service locations, zones, and airport pricing with promo codes and card, cash, and wallet payments.

Tech stack

A single API layer keeps pricing, dispatch, and trip state consistent across the website, the mobile apps, and the dashboard.

  • Laravel
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • REST API
  • JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Payments
  • Git

Architecture: an API-first Laravel backend serving the marketing site, the rider and driver apps, and a real-time operations dashboard, backed by a MySQL schema built around zones, trips, and fare rules.

Outcome

Amra RIDE is ready for its first market: a public site that explains the fair-earnings model, a rider and driver app pair launching on both stores, and an operations dashboard that covers everything from dispatch to driver approval and safety alerts.

Because every surface talks to the same API, the platform can expand into new cities and service zones without reworking the core.
